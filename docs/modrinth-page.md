# Better Advancements

A modern, clean replacement for the vanilla Advancements screen, with search,
sorting, categories, progress bars and an animated achievement banner.

## What it does

Vanilla shows your advancements as a tree of tabs that is hard to navigate once you
have dozens of them. This mod replaces that screen with a single organized panel:

- **Search** — find any advancement instantly by typing part of its name.
- **Sort** — by progress, alphabetical, or vanilla order.
- **Categories** — one click to jump to a tab (story, adventure, nether...).
- **Progress bars** — see at a glance how far you are on each advancement, with
  percentages.
- **Responsive grid** — 2 to 4 cards per row depending on your window size, with
  pagination.
- **Tooltips** — hover an advancement for its full description.

It also replaces the small toast in the top-right corner with an **animated banner
that slides in from the top center of the screen** when you unlock an advancement,
with a golden border for challenges.

## Details

- Client-side only: install it on your client and it works everywhere, including
  on servers.
- Works with modded advancements from other mods.

## Installation

Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API for your Minecraft version, then put the mod
jar in your `mods` folder. Press `L` (default key) to open the advancement browser.
